NerdStats

See how your Mac is really doing, right from the menu bar.

Download for macOS

macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple Silicon & Intel · Free

Opens the latest release on GitHub.

What you get

  • Everything at a glance. Live CPU, GPU, memory, storage, network and battery.
  • Temperatures and fans. Processor, graphics and battery heat, plus fan speeds.
  • Your menu bar, your way. Separate items for CPU, GPU, Memory, SSD and Battery, in any combination.
  • Plain-language overview. A simple Normal, Busy or Hot verdict for everyone.
  • Nerd mode. Per-core usage, load averages, every sensor and top processes, with Quit or Force Quit.
  • Network by app. Live connections per app with host, port, protocol and speed.
  • Quietly there. Starts at login and runs natively on Apple Silicon and Intel.

Install

  1. Open the downloaded NerdStats-<version>.dmg file.
  2. Drag NerdStats into your Applications folder.
  3. Open NerdStats. The first time, macOS will say it can't verify the app. Close that message, then open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to NerdStats. You only need to do this once.

Why the extra step? NerdStats is a free project without a paid Apple Developer account, so it isn't notarized by Apple. It's open source, so you can read every line of the code on GitHub or build it yourself.
